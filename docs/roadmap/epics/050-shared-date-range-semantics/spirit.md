# The spirit of this epic

A user can look at this month, this year, the last 30 or 365 days, or precisely March 5–17, wherever dates matter in VibeLedger. The app must not require rediscovering different date controls or carry different meanings of “rolling” across screens.

A From–To selection is always legitimate. A comparison is a separate question: compare these selected days to the corresponding dates last month, last year, or immediately preceding equal duration, or compare nothing. The labels must tell the truth about exactly which days are represented. Do not guess a comparison for a custom range.

Centralize date arithmetic and UI so the rest of the product reuses tested semantics. Preserve instant spending situational awareness and direct drilldowns; do not turn this into a general reporting framework or an AI feature.
