## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Before changing production reconciliation, add a frozen, deterministic test
  contract that distinguishes automatically confirmed pairs, review candidates,
  and unpaired transactions. Record the red baseline and literal expected pair
  states, transaction IDs, and accounting totals under
  `artifacts/PI-27-generic-transfer-reconciliation-tdd.md`.
- [ ] Define account-boundary eligibility generically: both sides must be posted
  transactions on two distinct, active linked accounts, with equal-and-opposite
  nonzero amounts, known equal currency, and dates within a small symmetric bound.
  Account institution, type, or subtype may corroborate evidence but cannot be a
  whitelist or the sole reason to confirm a pair.
- [ ] Pin high-confidence auto-confirmation to a mutually unique best match across
  the complete competing set plus strong independent transfer evidence. Accepted
  evidence must be structured and deterministic, such as coherent provider
  transfer/payment details on both legs or a previously confirmed, precisely
  defined account-pair signature; a user/category rule on one row, a generic word
  such as “payment,” or amount/date proximity alone is insufficient.
- [ ] Cover at least these positive shapes with literal fixtures: same-institution
  checking-to-savings, cross-institution checking-to-checking, checking-to-credit
  repayment with either posting order, and linked wallet/payment-account funding.
  Prove that the same reconciliation path handles them without per-product
  accounting branches.
- [ ] Cover ambiguity and false-positive controls: equally plausible inbound or
  outbound matches; a nearer unrelated collision; an ordinary purchase/refund or
  rent/payment sharing an amount; same-account reversal; one-sided external
  payment; one transfer-looking leg only; and a user-rejected pair. These cases
  must not be automatically excluded, and ties must not be broken by database or
  iteration order.
- [ ] Cover incomplete or changed evidence: pending rows, active duplicate
  corrections, unknown or different currency, zero/unequal amounts, fees, split or
  aggregated movements, dates outside the bound, inactive/unlinked history, a
  removed counterpart, and a provider modification after an automatic decision.
  The initial implementation may abstain on fee/split/FX cases rather than infer a
  many-to-many or converted transfer.
- [ ] Prove lifecycle behavior: detection runs during ordinary and historical sync,
  is idempotent, preserves valid manual confirmations, records why an automatic
  pair qualified, exposes that decision for audit, and allows unpair/rejection to
  restore both rows and prevent immediate automatic recreation. Automatically
  confirmed relationships whose source evidence becomes invalid are safely
  invalidated or reopened rather than silently retained.
- [ ] Prove accounting behavior through shared APIs: an auto-confirmed internal
  movement contributes zero household income and spending everywhere; a linked
  card purchase remains counted once; candidates and unmatched rows remain
  counted; Sankey and Activity expose the same pair state and transaction
  population.
- [ ] Freeze the reviewed tests. PI-28 and PI-29 may repair production code or add
  separate cases but may not alter this oracle without escalation.

## Technical Context

The current `app/services/transfer_detector.py` already indexes exact opposite
amounts and requires mutual closest matches, but it creates unconfirmed candidates
and gives reverse-order evidence special treatment only for checking-to-credit-card
repayments. `app/services/sync_service.py` invokes detection after ordinary and
historical imports; confirmed `TransferPair` rows drive the shared exclusion in
`app/api/routes.py`.

Design the oracle around a small evidence classifier and one-to-one reconciliation,
not category-name suppression or a broad account-type switch. Start with exact
one-to-one, same-currency movements. Do not require fuzzy amounts, FX conversion,
fee inference, split matching, or probabilistic scoring to claim acceptable recall;
those shapes should abstain until separately designed.

Existing protections in `tests/test_transfer_detector.py`,
`tests/test_pi19_card_payment_tdd.py`, and
`tests/test_pi20_card_payment_verifier.py` remain part of the contract. In
particular, replace the old documented “accepted limitation” only with a test-first
high-confidence rule that rejects the rent/payment collision.

Suggested role: accounting/test engineer who does not implement PI-28.
