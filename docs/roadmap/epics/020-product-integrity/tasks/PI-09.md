## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Document the role currently played by “Available cash” in normal surplus,
  exact-balance, deficit, refund-credit, refund-only, and zero-income cases using
  small reconciled examples.
- [ ] Explore at least two lightweight compositions that simplify the normal case
  without making refund credits look like income or hiding deficit funding.
- [ ] Compare the options on common-case visual simplicity, truthful exceptional
  cases, expansion/drilldown affordances, narrow-screen readability, arithmetic
  balance, and implementation/maintenance cost.
- [ ] Recommend one bounded design, including when exceptional nodes/companion
  explanations appear and what remains hidden in the ordinary case. “Delete the
  node” alone is not an adequate decision.
- [ ] Validate the recommendation against CF-14's identities and PI-06's actual
  card-payment diagnosis; do not alter accounting policy to make the picture easier.
- [ ] Record annotated sketches or concise rendered mockups and clearly distinguish
  design inference from user feedback. Raise only a choice that materially changes
  the product contract.

## Technical Context

The current React graph in `SankeyChart.tsx` routes Income, Net refund credits, and
Deficit funding into “Available cash,” then routes that hub to positive spending
buckets and Savings. `analytics_cashflow_sankey()` supplies positive net spend,
residual credits, savings, and deficit according to CF-14. Inspect its focused API
and component tests. PI-10 implements the accepted composition; no production code
belongs in this design task.

Suggested role: product designer/frontend engineer. Save the decision under
`artifacts/PI-09-sankey-composition.md` and link it here when complete.
