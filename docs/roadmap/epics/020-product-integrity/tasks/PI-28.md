inside `app/services/sync_service.py`, `TransferPair` persistence/migration in
`app/models/models.py` and `app/db/schema_patches.py`, transfer APIs in
`app/api/routes.py`, and relationship visibility in
`frontend/src/routes/transfers.tsx` and Activity annotations. Prefer a small named
evidence/result type over scattered boolean exceptions.

fee-aware matching, or many-to-many allocation. Those require separate product
contracts because they cannot meet the same automatic precision guarantee.
