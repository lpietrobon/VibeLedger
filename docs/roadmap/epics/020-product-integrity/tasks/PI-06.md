## Technical Context

`analytics_cashflow_sankey()` in `app/api/routes.py` already calls
`posted_activity(..., include_transfers=False)` and sums the shared
`expense_amount()` expression from `app/services/accounting.py`. A category such as
Plaid `LOAN_PAYMENTS` maps to `FINANCE/LOANS`; displayed wording may also reflect a
manual or rule category. Transfer state lives in `TransferPair`, and Activity exposes
`is_transfer` versus `is_transfer_candidate`. Existing tests prove exclusion for
monthly/cashflow endpoints but do not explicitly assert the linked-card case against
the Sankey category payload.
