# The spirit of this epic

VibeLedger should let me look at my spending and trust what it says. As I connect
more checking accounts, credit cards, and payment accounts such as Venmo, the
picture should become more complete without counting the same money twice. A
jump in reported spending should reflect a real change in my expenses, and I
should be able to understand that change without auditing the entire ledger.

The central questions are simple: Where do this month's expenses stand compared
with last month? How is spending developing over the year? What categories and
subscriptions explain it? Which transactions genuinely deserve another look?
Income and expenses should be understandable together, with enough evidence to
explain the totals and enough honesty to show when the picture is uncertain.

All linked accounts form the boundary of this view. Moving money between them
does not itself create income or spending. Purchases count when posted, and paying
off a linked credit card must not count the purchase again. New account history
and corrections should improve past reports. Uncertainty must stay visible rather
than quietly removing a potentially real expense.

This work strengthens what already exists. It should reduce bookkeeping effort,
keep categories manageable, and make reviewing questionable transactions useful.
The results must be reproducible and explainable without an AI interpreting the
whole ledger. Investments and broader financial optimization can wait. Success is
a clear, dependable cashflow picture that remains simple enough for one person to
maintain, with focused tests that protect that confidence as the project grows.
