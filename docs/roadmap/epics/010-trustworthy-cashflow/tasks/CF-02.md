## Technical Context

Suggested role: accounting/domain analyst paired with an engineer. This is a
reporting contract, not financial advice or a new double-entry bookkeeping system.
Use the user's linked-account boundary and posting-date choices. Pending activity
does not belong in realized totals. Distinguish a merchant refund from unlinked
person-to-person reimbursement; do not introduce mandatory manual matching.

Keep examples small enough to audit by hand. CF-04 turns them into regression
fixtures, and CF-10 independently checks the final results against these oracles.
The worked examples can live in this task body or a linked test fixture.
